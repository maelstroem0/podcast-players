## Adam Parker — Full Participant Brief

### 1) Firm & role — Trivariate Research
- **Trivariate Research LP** — independent equity-research shop, NY (950 Third Ave, Suite 2500). Founder & CEO = Adam Parker. Sell-side-style **subscription research to institutions** (fundamental + quant + macro combined). ✅
- **Business model:** subscription tiers → access to model outputs, written research, themed events, conference calls/webcasts, research library. Plus **bespoke institutional work**: customized projects, *outsourced risk management & portfolio diagnostics*, 1-on-1s. Revenue = subscriptions + custom consulting, NOT AUM. ✅
- **Does NOT run money.** Site lists no fund, ETF, or investable model portfolio as a product. Team is analysts + research-sales (Colin Cooney head of sales, Ryan McGovern, Maxwell Arnold, Chang Ge, Jesse Goodman). ✅
- **Founding date:** ⚠️ Firm launched ~**2020** (post-Eminence); exact date UNVERIFIED from primary source — treat "founded ~2020" as approximate.
- **Adjacent launch (2025):** spun up **Trivector Research** — a retail/advisor-facing publication ("making market insights consumable for advisors and individuals"). Broadens the franchise downstream of institutions. ✅
- **Skin in the game:** his "book" is the **research subscription franchise + TV reputation**, not a position book. He previously DID run a fund (see §2) — so he can trade — but there's no disclosed live fund now. Key conflict distinction vs. a hedge-fund guest (§4).

### 2) Career track record
- **Education:** 3 statistics degrees — Michigan (BA), UNC-Chapel Hill (MS Biostatistics), Boston University (PhD). Quant by training. ✅
- **Sanford Bernstein (1999–):** started as quant researcher → **fundamental semiconductor analyst**. II-ranked. Semis in his DNA — relevant to this episode's chip focus. ✅
- **Morgan Stanley, Chief US Equity Strategist + Dir. Global Quant Research (2010–early 2017):** multiple-time II-ranked strategist. Replaced by **Mike Wilson** (then Wealth Mgmt CIO) in 2017. ✅
- **Buy-side (2017–2019):** Dir. of Quantitative Strategy at **Eminence Capital** (L/S equity, Ricky Sandler). Then founder/lead PM of **Trivariate Capital**, an equity **long/short hedge fund** — before pivoting the "Trivariate" brand to pure research. ✅ *(So the man has run real risk; he is not only ever a talking head.)*
- **Big-call scorecard (MS years):**
  - **Bearish / wrong 2012–2013:** carried a **too-low multiple**. Nov-2012 note set YE-2013 target **1,434** (bull 1,733 / bear 1,135); S&P ran to ~1,560, forcing a mid-2013 hike to **1,600**. His own mea culpa: *"Only the hubristic deign to project the market multiple… as evidently we did this year in projecting another year of multiple contraction."* ✅ Reputation in that era = **cautious/bearish strategist**.
  - Claims 2011 was a year he "accidentally got the multiple right." ⚠️ Self-reported, UNVERIFIED independently.
  - **Turned bullish by Jan 2017** ("big inflection point") just before departing. ✅
- **Net read:** rigorous quant, candid about being wrong, *skews cautious/contrarian by temperament* but is presently one of the more **bullish** Street voices (see §3). Not a permabear, not a permabull — regime-switcher.

### 3) Public positioning 2025–2026 (dense)
- **S&P 500 target: BULLISH ~8,000** (CNBC 2026-07-07). Math: 20× 2027 consensus EPS **$401 = 8,020**; on Trivariate's own EPS **~$386** at **22× = 8,492**. S&P already +~10% YTD 2026 (4th straight up year). ✅
- **Concentration/AI framing:** *"the S&P 500 is an AI factor bet"*; quality-adj AI factor at **highest-ever correlation** to the index. Tech = **~59% of expected earnings expansion next 2 yrs** ("incredibly high"). Top 5–7 names (Micron/Nvidia) ≈ **45% of Q1 earnings growth**. ✅
- **Mag 7 stance:** counsels institutions to stay **market-weight** Mag 7 (low idiosyncratic risk, over-covered) rather than under/over-weight. ✅
- **Semiconductors: constructive; "semis over software."** ✅
  - **NVDA** still favored (CUDA moat, hyperscaler capex durability).
  - **Micron (MU)** a headline pick — memory/DRAM AI beneficiary (~7× fwd EPS), crossed **$1T** cap; but flags **long-term DRAM oversupply / margin compression** as the risk. Asks whether the semis valuation "chasm is too wide."
- **Software: his single "highest-conviction" AVOID.** Median software squeezed — must spend on AI to defend 80% gross margins → pricing pushback → sales misses. **Cheap software = value trap** ("low valuations reflect justified pessimism"). Exception: high-growth security w/ switching costs — **PANW, CRWD**. ✅
- **Healthcare: highest-conviction OUT-OF-CONSENSUS OVERWEIGHT.** Thesis: aging demographics + AI diagnostics + political will → spend > GDP; *"healthcare is where we'll see most productivity benefits from AI."* Names/areas: **drug distributors, managed care, Quest (DGX), LabCorp (LH)**; wearable-enabled preventive medicine. Trivariate lists have also flagged **Abbott, AbbVie, Stryker**. ✅
- **Diversification / non-tech tilt (recurring 2025–26 CNBC theme):** build an **AI back-up plan** — dividend-*growth* stocks and quality non-tech growers as ballast if AI trade stumbles. ✅
- **AI reality-check nuance:** only **~9% of 3,000 US public equities** have meaningful AI *revenue* (16% cite cost benefit) → *"in the next year… we better see some serious productivity."* Bullish index, skeptical on breadth of monetization. ✅

### 4) Conflicts / incentives — characterize honestly
- **NOT a book-talker.** No disclosed live fund/AUM; he does not benefit from you buying MU/NVDA into an event. Different animal from a hedge-fund pod guest selling into a position.
- **Real incentive = subscription franchise + airtime.** An independent research shop monetizes by being **differentiated, provocative, quotable** → sells subscriptions and stays on CNBC/podcasts. Incentive to have a **loud, non-consensus flag** (his healthcare OW; his "avoid software" call are exactly that). Bias vector = **memorability/contrarianism**, not position-pumping.
- **Provider-of-risk-services angle:** Trivariate sells outsourced risk mgmt & portfolio diagnostics to funds — mild incentive to frame the world as complex/needing-a-quant (i.e., to sell the tooling). Low-grade, disclose-able.
- **Legacy long/short PM instincts** — thinks in factor exposures & pair trades (semis-over-software, market-weight-Mag7), which is analytically honest but means his "calls" are relative/factor bets, not clean directional trades.
- **Disclosed personal positions:** none found. ⚠️ UNVERIFIED (independent shop, no 13F filing obligation; no position disclosures located).
- **Net:** treat his views as **reputation-weighted analysis**, not a conflicted pitch. Discount for showmanship/contrarian-signaling, not for talking-his-book.

His "book" = the MacroVoices platform + newsletter/research; occasionally promotes his energy/gold structural theses. ✅
