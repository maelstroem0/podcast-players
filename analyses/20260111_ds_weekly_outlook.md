## Executive Summary

Trump's two rate initiatives—**10% credit card cap** and **$200B Fannie/Freddie mortgage buying**—signal affordability thrust targeting banks. Mortgage spreads dropped 15-18bps as expected (headline rate to 5.99%), but real impact is **house prices bid up, not affordability improved**. Fannie/Freddie can add $200B to balance sheet but historically run matched books—paying swaps, buying swaption convexity. If they run naked duration instead, that's **private-sector QE** removing duration from market. Swap spreads at least-negative levels consistent with Fannie becoming payers. Credit card cap (no legal authority) would cut bank profitability → reduced credit → consumption hit. **Short banks** into earnings—affordability thrust puts them center of target.

**Curve flattening Friday** was positioning squeeze, not trend break. Twos sold off 9bps, 10s30s flattened 5bps—first 10s30s move in stable segment. Overloaded boat on steepeners/weak dollar got squeezed on SCOTUS delay + Trump mortgage comments. Andy wrong on long twos/STIR so far—terminal rate now 3.16-3.17% (two cuts). But Trump Fed (Myran at 2.125% dot) hasn't backed off dovish stance despite extolling 5% GDP. Watch Myran's comments—administration mouthpiece.

**Gold/silver constructive** near all-time highs but Andy interested in shorting gold on rips—RMOs end April, DS Protect has crushed it. Oil held 56, bounced to 59—technical damage to upside. Admin wants 55-65 band ($2.75-3.00 gasoline). No fundamental "why" for Friday's bounce—backdoor deals suspected. **Equity vol and bond vol dirt cheap**—must own puts whether hedging or alpha. Calls also cheap. JHEQX collar levels: buy at 10 (cost butterfly 2.5pts below market) and at 2 (put butterfly).

**PM implication:** Short banks into earnings—affordability thrust makes them target. Long twos/STIR as scale trade (35% risk deployed). Short 10Y via puts. Long yen at 159.5. Sell gold on rips. Buy oil below 56 only. Add Europe/Japan risk parity—non-US attractive. Vol is cheap both ways—own equity puts, bond puts, and cheap calls as insurance.
