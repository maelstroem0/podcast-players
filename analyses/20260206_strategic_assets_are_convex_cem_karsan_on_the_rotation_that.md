Industrial metals, precious metals, lithium, defense tech, chips—anything with bottlenecks or concentrated in specific countries. This is a 10-15 year trend. (2) **Demand-side pivot**—Trump 2.0 attempted supply-side (tax cuts, deregulation, synthetic QE via shortened curve) but political winds forced reversal. Millennials born post-1982 have only experienced 43 years of inequality, globalization, falling rates—household formation and wealth at 45% of boomers at same age. Populism is structural, not cyclical. What worked last year (tech, consumer discretionary) got crushed; what died last year (consumer staples, defensives) exploded as markets price fiscal spending into midterms, curve steepening, and inflation breakevens.

Karsan's 62-82 parallel: presidential years averaged +21.5% (all five double-digit), midterm years averaged -8.5% (every one had 20%+ drawdown, one hit -45%). Politics only matter during populist periods—outside 62-82, presidential cycles show no edge. 2026 is a midterm year with debt refinancing wave (2021 refi's rolling at 5% not 0.5%), fiscal stimulus colliding with liquidity drain. "Shooting the generals" (leadership turnover) is bearish for indices but bullish for rotation winners. Venezuela arrest was about cutting China's energy supply, not democracy. Iran likely gets peace deal not regime change—Middle East realignment (Saudi-Turkey-Qatar alliance opposing Israeli overreach) removes leverage US thought it had. Market pricing military conflict; Karsan says fade that, buy the dip when peace announced, but use it to reload strategic assets for decade-long move.

**PM implication:** Long strategic assets with convexity—industrial/precious metals, defense tech, semis, concentrated supply chains. Rotate from tech/mag seven into consumer staples, defensives, anything left for dead in 2025. Steepeners NOW—buy long-end protection, curve steepening is real (fiscal + debt refi wave). Watch housing policy for populist tipping point (first-time buyer checks = velocity one inflation). Fade Iran war premium short-term (peace deal likely), use relief rally to add commodity/hard asset exposure. Underweight US equity multiples (higher long rates = compression), overweight thematic rotation plays. This is Year 1 of a 10-15 year populism/conflict/deglobalization cycle—62-82 rhyme means -20%+ drawdown likely this year, but strategic assets compound through it.
