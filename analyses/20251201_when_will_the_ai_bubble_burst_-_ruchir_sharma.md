## Executive Summary

Sharma frames the US economy as **one big bet on AI**—60% of economic growth driven by AI capex (40% direct) plus wealth effect (top 10% spending powered by AI stock gains). The bubble checklist is filling: **overinvestment** (tech capex at 5% of GDP, matching 2000), **overvaluation** (price-to-FCF at extremes), **overownership** (52% of US household wealth in equities, higher than 2000), and **overleveraging now accelerating** as Meta/Amazon/MSFT issue debt for AI arms race. The trigger for bursting: **interest rates going up**—every bubble in history pricked by same factor.

The key distinction from prior tech bubbles: **this is the most hated tech revolution**. Only 35% of people feel positive about AI; most fear job displacement. China's DeepSeek moment showed convergence at 1/5 the capex spend ($100B vs $500B), but US markets shrugged it off—faith in AI remains unquestioned. Meanwhile, **quality stocks have had one of the worst 12-month runs in recorded history**, creating a contrarian entry.

For PMs: **underweight US, overweight international**—this outperformance trend is multi-year once it begins. Rotate into quality factor (high ROE, low leverage, reasonable valuations) which is historically cheap. The bubble won't burst until rates rise, and Fed keeps cutting despite 3% inflation—but watch for any hawkish pivot as the signal to exit AI longs. This is a "good bubble" (productivity-enhancing), but good bubbles still burst.

## 2) Key Investable Insights

### Insight 1: Quality Factor at Historic Discount

- **Claim:** Quality stocks (high ROE, low leverage, stable growth) have had one of the worst 12-month periods in recorded history—contrarian entry point.
- **Asset Class:** Global Equities (Factor)
- **Mechanism:** AI mania drove speculative flows to momentum/growth; quality ignored. Mean reversion plus defensive characteristics when bubble bursts.
- **Expression:** Long quality factor ETFs (QUAL, SPHQ) or custom screen (ROE >15%, debt/equity sub-0.5x, 3yr earnings stability).
- **Trigger to Exit/Flip:** Quality outperforms for 6+ months; valuation gap closes.
- **Confidence:** High

### Insight 2: International Outperformance is Multi-Year

- **Claim:** US underperformed international in 2025 (Europe, EM, China all beat); this is the start of a multi-year trend, not a blip.
- **Asset Class:** Global Equities (Geographic)
- **Mechanism:** US at 70% MSCI weight (extreme crowding), dollar overvalued; Europe/China doing reforms under pressure (Germany labor markets, China pivoting to private sector for AI).
- **Expression:** Underweight US (reduce SPY), overweight EAFE/EM (VEA, VWO, FXI).
- **Trigger to Exit/Flip:** US relative valuations normalize (sub-50% MSCI weight); dollar fair value.
- **Confidence:** Medium-High

### Insight 3: AI Bubble Bursts on Rate Hikes

- **Claim:** All bubbles burst when rates rise. Fed still cutting despite 3% inflation—bubble intact until Fed pivots hawkish.
- **Asset Class:** US Equities, Rates
- **Mechanism:** AI capex financed by debt (Meta, AMZN, MSFT issuing); higher rates = higher cost of capital = multiple compression + capex slowdown.
- **Expression:** Short QQQ puts as hedge; long 2y Treasuries as rate-up play if inflation re-accelerates.
- **Trigger to Exit/Flip:** Fed signals pause/hike cycle; 10y above 5%.
- **Confidence:** Medium (timing uncertain)

## 4) Regime & Scenario Analysis

- **Current Regime Fit:** Late-cycle bubble + fiscal/monetary stimulus. Classic "good bubble" (tech/productivity) but still a bubble.
- **Base Case (55%):** AI bubble deflates slowly over 2026 as Fed pauses; quality/international outperform; no crash, just rotation.
- **Alt Case (30%):** Inflation re-accelerates → Fed hikes → bubble bursts sharply; 20-30% drawdown in US tech; flight to quality and international.
- **Bull Case (15%):** AI productivity gains materialize faster than expected; capex justified; bubble becomes new normal.
- **Anti-Thesis Trigger:** Productivity data shows clear AI impact (sub-2yr); Fed achieves soft landing at 2% inflation.
- **Tail Risks:** China DeepSeek 2.0 moment (AI parity at fraction of cost); US fiscal crisis forces Fed hand.
