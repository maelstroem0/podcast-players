# Participants & Conflict Audit — "Did The Bust Just Begin?"
## Thoughtful Money / Adam Taggart — Jesse Felder — Published 2026-06-11

---

## Conflict Summary

Both participants carry material financial incentives aligned with their stated bearish/commodities-bullish thesis. Jesse Felder operates a paid subscription service ($195/yr) whose core value proposition is contrarian, bearish macro analysis — his business requires a perpetual bear market narrative to justify subscriber retention. He has been consistently early or wrong on US equity top calls (predicted recession and bear market return by fall 2023 — S&P finished +16%), yet his stated personal tilt (long gold/silver miners, long energy/oil) is precisely what he recommends subscribers buy, creating a direct talking-their-book conflict with no disclosed position transparency. Adam Taggart's Thoughtful Money LLC is a registered solicitor for financial advisory firms (primarily New Harbor Financial Group and Lance Roberts/RIA Advisors), receiving up to **35% of ongoing AUM advisory fees** from clients referred through his platform — a structural incentive to feature bearish guests who motivate audience members to seek professional portfolio protection. Neither conflict is hidden, but neither is foregrounded in episode framing.

**Summary**: Felder has a long track record of directionally correct secular-cycle calls (commodities over US equities, precious metals supercycle) but a poor record on near-term timing, having called tops and recessions 1–3 years early on multiple occasions. His 2023 recession prediction was a clean miss. His contrarian positioning in commodities/energy/gold has performed well in absolute terms during the 2024–2026 Iran-shock/oil-spike environment.

## Adam Taggart / Thoughtful Money — Incentives

### Background
- Brown University undergraduate → investment banker at Merrill Lynch → Stanford MBA → Silicon Valley startup (acquired) → Head of Marketing / VP Marketing at Yahoo! Finance.
- **Co-Founder & President, Peak Prosperity** (June 2012 – April 2021) — known for doomer/prepper-adjacent macro content alongside Chris Martenson.
- **Founder & CEO, Wealthion** (April 2021 – October 2023) — similar macro-bearish interview platform.
- **Founder & CEO, Thoughtful Money** (2023–present) — latest iteration; same bearish macro interview format, new branding.
- Sources: [Thoughtful Money About](https://thoughtfulmoney.com/about/), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/adamtaggart/)

### Structural Conflict: RIA Solicitor Model
- **Thoughtful Money LLC is a "Registered Investment Advisor Promoter"** (its own language on the About page) and a formal **Solicitor** under SEC rules for unaffiliated RIAs.
- **Endorsed advisors include**: New Harbor Financial Group (CRD# 155528) and Lance Roberts / RIA Advisors.
- **Compensation**: Thoughtful Money receives **up to 35% of ongoing AUM advisory fees** charged to referred clients, paid by the RIA within 30 days of each fee collection. This is a *recurring* fee — not a one-time referral bounty — meaning every year a referred client stays with New Harbor, Taggart receives 35% of that client's advisory fee.
- **Scale**: Taggart has stated his platform has directed "over half a billion dollars of investment capital" into professional management. At a standard 1% AUM fee and 35% solicitor cut, this implies ~$1.75M/year in solicitor income at steady state (rough estimate).
- **Disclosed**: This is disclosed in Thoughtful Money's Form ADV Part 2A and Form CRS regulatory filings, and in Solicitor Disclosure Statements given to referred clients. It is NOT prominently disclosed in YouTube episode descriptions or at the start of podcasts.
- **Conflict**: Guests who express bearish views and recommend professional portfolio protection directly serve Taggart's revenue model. There is no financial benefit to having a guest say "you don't need an advisor."
- Sources: [Thoughtful Money About page](https://thoughtfulmoney.com/about/), [ADV Part 2A Oct 2024 filing (PDF)](https://thoughtfulmoney.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Thoughtful-Money-LLC-Part-2A.pdf), confirmed 35% solicitor rate via regulatory disclosure search
