## Executive Summary

The 2026 market regime is a **compressed-vol pain trade**: equities grind lower in stair-steps, VIX stays suppressed, and every instrument people use to hedge — puts, bonds, precious metals — loses value into declines. Carson explains the mechanical architecture: when vol is well-supplied and the world is broadly hedged, **Vega doesn't work**. Implied vol gets crushed on the way down (skew steepens, realized moves happen, but your options P&L is flat or negative). This is the same regime as 2022, and it creates a trap where retail buys every dip on calls, and institutions can't monetize their hedges. The result: a slow melt with no cleanV-bottom to pivot from.

The **Thursday–Monday decay pattern** is the actionable discovery. Carson predicted this in January before Iran escalation: 150% of annual declines would concentrate from Thursday midday to Monday open. The mechanism is structural — vol compression is so extreme that the only mechanism to release risk is unhedgeable event risk (weekend geopolitical news). During the week, speculator shorts get squeezed on Monday morning as vol compresses back. This has played out five consecutive weeks. The administration understands this, which is why they pushed intervention tweets on the sixth week (today) going into a known geopolitical weekend.

The rates view is the structural anchor: 10-year to **7–8% within 2–3 years**. Carson made the same call from zero in 2021 to 5%. The steepener is "an incredible trade that will continue." The mechanism: equity yield (~4%) vs. 10yr creates a hard ceiling — if 10yr crosses 5%, institutions are forced to rotate, deleveraging private equity and credit collateral chains. The Fed is in a box (can't fight inflation without raising rates), and the supply-demand imbalance for bonds is so large that Treasury intervention has limits. Geopolitical escalation (Iran/China proxy dynamics) is inflationary structurally for the next 10–15 years — oil, supply chains, fiscal spending.

**PM implication:** Stay short Vega (sell monthly puts, buy zero-DTE gamma for realized moves). Steepeners — long 30yr, short 2–5yr — remain the highest-conviction structural trade. Fade the Monday morning vol compression squeezes. Avoid precious metals as a hedge here (gets sucked into the vortex). Watch 10yr at 5% as the equity stress trigger. Zero-DTE long gamma is the correct tactical expression in this regime, not Vega.
