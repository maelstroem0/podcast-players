## A. Key Quotes (verbatim, 12 selected)

1. **[~1:40] Adam Parker:** "You know, I don't really talk to that many people who think it's a bubble. Maybe I'm in a bubble, but... I think the corporate earnings have been strong and the price to earnings probably likely contract some. And so you're left with a choppy but upward trending US equity market for at least the next 6-12 months just because of the strength of the underlying earnings."

2. **[~2:30] Adam Parker:** "I mean, look, people have called for 17 of the last zero, you know, problems with their earnings trajectory."

3. **[~3:15] Adam Parker:** "One of the main dynamics in the last year has been the penalty for missing earnings has been way harsher than the reward for beating earnings. So if you're running a diversified portfolio of equities trying to beat the S&P, you just cannot own stuff that misses."

4. **[~3:45] Adam Parker:** "Because the S&P is such an AI semiconductor ETF, having energy stocks at a 40-year low on their correlation to the tech sector, I think is a nice diversifying feature as well. So I kind of like owning more than the bench weight in energy in a US equity book."

5. **[~5:00] Adam Parker:** "When I was an analyst, Nvidia was too small to cover by market cap and liquidity, if you can believe that."

6. **[~5:15] Adam Parker:** "Currently about 265 of the top 3,000 US equities by market cap have meaningful AI revenue, which we define as 5% or more of their revenue or a new product announcement indicating that's imminent. When you do that, every sector is recommended except for consumer staples. All of the [Mag] Eight except for Apple have meaningful AI revenue."

7. **[~6:15] Adam Parker on Micron:** "We did 10,000 simulations varying the periodicity and amplitude of their earnings cycle... came to the conclusion that the stock trades around four to five times peak earnings and 10 times normalized earnings right now — we think that's probably a little bit too cheap."

8. **[~8:45] Adam Parker:** "China's got their act together better on energy than we do... I'm not so sure of the underlying fundamentals there, but there are definitely ways to play it within the US public market."

9. **[~11:00] Adam Parker on healthcare:** "The market's telling us that there's a 0% chance healthcare is the best performing sector in the market over the next 5 years, and I think it's like a 30 or 40% chance and I want to arb that difference... Healthcare revenue per share has grown every year 30-plus years in a row."

10. **[~14:00] Adam Parker on rates:** "When I worked at Morgan Stanley, they were wrong every single year forecasting interest rates. Every year I worked there, they were wrong. And if I impregnated their rate view into my equity view, it would have not been helpful."

11. **[~19:30] Adam Parker on Hormuz/oil impact:** "We looked at every earnings call transcript... through that April earning season... for any commentary from the top S&P 500 companies on Iran and on oil... We came out with a view that it really wasn't [going to have a big impact]... it wasn't going to have that big of a net impact on earnings."

12. **[~23:00] Adam Parker on portfolio construction:** "Our conclusion was pretty strong that really since 2020 you had to run more diversified than normal. That the drawdowns from concentrated portfolios — even for 75th percentile stock pickers — have been too extreme."

13. **[~26:00] Adam Parker on value investing:** "I don't really think valuation works to pick stocks. Meaning like if I buy cheap stocks and short expensive stocks, I make zero money — and I haven't made any money doing that for 15 years."

14. **[~26:45] Adam Parker:** "What does work actually... is buying stocks that just got more expensive and avoiding stocks that just got cheaper... the probability you beat a second time given you beat a first time is higher than the unconditional probability. So all of a sudden it's not just momentum — it's actually there's information in it."

15. **[~30:30] Adam Parker on quality investing:** "The way we computed high quality actually hasn't beaten junk for six years now... about 82% of the current S&P 500 market cap is top half quality. So a reasonable question for a long investor trying to beat the S&P 500 would be: do you need to be overweight to 82%?"

16. **[~33:00] Adam Parker on AI in his own research:** "We use Claude and analyzed all of my Sunday level-sets for the last three and a half years to see if there's any subsequent one-week predictive power in the language I was using... the answer was there was... some predictive value."

17. **[~35:30] Adam Parker on quant/multistrat scale:** "They might have 30 quant teams at one of these multistrats. The quant teams might run 50 to 150 million each... the holding period of these is usually 3 hours to 10 days with a median of about 5 days. So almost all quant money these days is run with a 20-30% daily turnover."

18. **[~38:00] Adam Parker on the fundamentals-vs-quant split:** "Quant is probably helpful for purely for like a third of equities, probably quant plus fundamental combined is probably good for about a third, then pure fundamentals is good for a third."
