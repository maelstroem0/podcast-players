## Executive Summary

Wilson's framework inverts conventional Fed priorities: **funding the deficit first, jobs second, inflation third**. The Fed ended QT early and started $40B/month asset purchases in December—a surprise nobody had called. But crypto's muted response signals just enough liquidity, not excess. New Fed chair (post-May) creates volatility regardless of who's nominated—markets test every new chair at their first presser. Bond vigilante risk lies in **30-year yields, not 10s**—Treasury's bill-heavy issuance starves duration demand, compressing 10Y by 70-80bps. Dollar weakness is the early warning for emerging-market-style funding stress.

**Employment bottomed April 2025**—Wilson's out-of-consensus call now validated. No hiring, no firing = margins explode. Productivity at 4% (6-year high) reflects GDP calculation quirks + immigration suppression + AI threat, not yet AI reality. He's modeling 40bps AI productivity contribution—but the **threat of AI is changing corporate behavior** before tools are deployed. White-collar firms won't rush to hire even as revenue picks up. The rolling recession in goods/industrials is over; services-to-goods spending shift benefits S&P (more goods-sensitive).

**Broadening thesis activated November 2025**: cyclical, higher operating leverage, lower quality stocks outperform for cyclical reasons, not secular. Equal-weight ETFs beat cap-weight across sectors. Healthcare (biotech M&A), consumer goods (gasoline down 16% YoY, tax benefits), energy services (Venezuela capex), and mid-cap industrials all set up. European outperformance since Liberation Day is exhausted—US resumes leadership in local and dollar terms.

**S&P 7,800 target** (vs 6,900 current). Bull case 8,600-9,000 if 20%+ earnings growth priced as perpetual—that's 99-style meltup risk. Breath/index divergence shows 10-12% gap; either breath improves or 5-10% correction resolves it. Watch dollar-yen below 150 for 5% drawdown risk. Investment grade issuance $250B in January (2x 10-year average)—capital markets wide open except for equity IPOs where sellers reject current prices.

### Insight 1: Broadening Thesis—Equal Weight Beats Cap Weight
- **Claim:** Cyclical, higher operating leverage, lower quality stocks outperform as rolling recession ends and earnings growth broadens
- **Asset Class:** US Equities
- **Mechanism:** April employment trough = new cycle beginning; revenue accelerating without hiring = margin expansion in mid-tier names; cap-weighted indices concentrated in MAG7 which has stalled

### Insight 2: Services-to-Goods Spending Shift
- **Claim:** Consumer spending rotating from services (hotels, restaurants) to goods—benefits S&P 500 which is goods-sensitive
- **Asset Class:** US Equities (Consumer)
- **Mechanism:** White-collar job insecurity (AI threat) + immigration-driven service wage inflation; gasoline -16% YoY = purchasing power; consumer goods upgraded first time in 4 years
- **Expression:** Long consumer goods (XLP constituents); avoid hotels, restaurants, experiential services; home improvement over homebuilders
- **Trigger to Exit/Flip:** Service sector employment stabilizes; gasoline prices spike
- **Confidence:** Medium-High

### Insight 3: 30-Year Yields as Bond Market Stress Signal
- **Claim:** 30Y spot is the true measure of term premium concern—10Y artificially compressed 70-80bps by Treasury's bill-heavy issuance
- **Asset Class:** US Rates
- **Mechanism:** Treasury starving market of duration = natural buyers bid 10Y; 30Y less liquid, less demand, more volatile; dollar weakness = emerging market funding pattern
- **Expression:** Watch 30Y yield for widening; hedge via TLT puts if 30Y breaks higher; monitor DXY for weakness confirmation
- **Trigger to Exit/Flip:** Treasury extends duration; recession risk rises (then bonds rally)
- **Confidence:** Medium

### Insight 4: Dollar-Yen Below 150 = 5% Drawdown Risk
- **Claim:** Yen at 157 solves problems; move to low 150s triggers carry unwind and 5% equity correction
- **Asset Class:** FX, Equities
- **Mechanism:** Japanese money repatriating from treasuries to JGBs; BOJ raising rates more aggressively; carry trade unwind risk persists despite repeated false alarms
- **Expression:** Watch USD/JPY; hedge via yen longs or SPY puts if approaching 150
- **Trigger to Exit/Flip:** USD/JPY stabilizes above 155
- **Confidence:** Medium
