Deflationary impulse real but based on lagged rent calculations + oil geopolitics, not structural demand destruction. Employment data reliability questioned (initial claims accuracy, post-COVID collection changes).

**Unchallenged Assumptions**

Mag 7 concentration risk universally accepted—no debate on whether rotation actually impossible. Bond vigilantes matter—assumed selloff triggers equity contagion (what if decoupling?). Fed chair pick moves markets—already priced? Waller "best case" may be consensus. Tariff ruling as binary—ignores messy middle (partial injunction, phased rollback).
