## Speaker Bios

**Andy Constan** is founder and CIO of Damped Spring Advisors. Former head of portfolio strategy at Bridgewater Associates and derivatives trader at Salomon Brothers. Known for volatility-adjusted macro positioning and cross-asset transmission frameworks.

**Jimmy** is a technical analyst and regular contributor to DS Weekly Outlook. Specializes in market structure, options flow, and short-term tactical setups.

## Executive Summary

Andy executes a **full rotation out of US assets** at Monday's open—the culmination of a year-long shift from 100% US beta to majority rest-of-world. The thesis: US equities and bonds are rich, financial conditions tightening, and **every non-dollar asset has outperformed** (gold, silver, EM, Europe). Meanwhile, **STIR pricing <1 cut over 4 meetings is a bargain**—he sees June cut as near-certain regardless of Fed chair replacement noise.

The tactical setup is a **"taco" (Trump tariff reversal) → gap fill → fail**. Jimmy notes Trump "broke the charts"—NASDAQ through yearly lows, creating overhead resistance even on a resolution. The steepener trade that dominated last week got squeezed; now 2s10s is spiking back. Critical observation: **bonds dumped on a risk-off day**, which should have helped them. Andy attributes this to global back-end repricing (JGB selling) rather than deficit fears, with flows visibly moving from UST to JGBs.

**Metals remain bullish**—consolidation patterns are "refreshers not tops." Jimmy sees silver targets at $125. No positions to short yet; just reduce from overweight to regular weight. Energy (crude) is range-bound at $58.50 floor until Iran headlines clear; fish behind the boat with $57 bids. Bitcoin acted weird on Sunday open—down to $92K despite gold/silver strength—but still making higher highs/lows.

**PM implication:** Exit US equity/bond beta at open. Own ROW/EM instead. Buy STIR (Feb ZN 111 puts look attractive; look to sell 110.5s or 110s on a squeeze to 111.13-111.15 support). On taco rip, sell gap fill—bonds won't participate, curve will get heavy and drag equities back down. Metals: trim to neutral, not short. Watch 10Y 4.30-4.40 as next resistance; ZN 110.20 as major support for bond longs.
