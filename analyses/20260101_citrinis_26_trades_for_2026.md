## Executive Summary

The **"BS Jobs" thesis** anchors 2026's highest-conviction trade: long companies with bloated white-collar headcount that can massively cut via AI—Accenture, Marsh McLennan, ADP, Paychex. This isn't hiring freeze; it's **active workforce reduction enabled by LLMs**. When a company reports flat revenue with 15% fewer employees, margins explode. The gap between AI capability and corporate utilization remains wide—that's the alpha. First movers who deploy AI for actual cost reduction outperform; skeptics waiting for "proof" miss the repricing.

**Advanced packaging** emerges as the structural bottleneck for all AI silicon—whether Nvidia, Google TPUs, or Chinese ASICs. Intel's packaging division (Intel Foundry Services), Amkor, Synopsis, and BESI benefit regardless of who wins the chip design race. CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate) capacity determines AI compute scaling more than fab capacity. Memory semis remain mid-cycle with HBM shortage intact, but watch for glut signals.

The **"Post-Traumatic Supply Disorder" (PTSD) framework** explains why gas turbines and memory semis crushed 2025: companies traumatized by prior overcapacity cycles refuse to add supply despite surging demand. GE Vernova targeting 20% EBIT margins (from 14%) because they won't build new factories despite doubled demand projections. Screen for: oligopoly structure + demand surge + capital discipline + prior trauma. Solar (First Solar), lithium (SQM, Albemarle), and analog semis fit the screen for 2026.

**Google TPU vs Nvidia** is false binary—both can win. MediaTek (20x P/E) designs next-gen TPUs and benefits from inference-on-device shift. ACMR trades at 5x discount to its Chinese A-share equivalent—arbitrage unhedgeable but valuation gap should close. China's semiconductor buildout continues regardless of success—don't fight CCP capex. AI mid-cycle confirmed: OpenAI raising $100B means capex boom persists. Bubble indicators to watch: broad retail euphoria, inventory air pockets, 12-month adoption stall.

## 2) Key Investable Insights

### Insight 1: BS Jobs / AI Workforce Reduction
- **Claim:** Companies with bloated white-collar headcount (consultants, insurance brokers, HR services) can cut 15-30% via AI deployment, driving massive margin expansion
- **Asset Class:** US Equities (Services)
- **Mechanism:** LLMs replace knowledge workers at scale; hiring freeze = net cuts; revenue flat + headcount down 15% = EPS explosion
- **Expression:** Long ACN, MMC, ADP, PAYX; avoid companies already lean or hardware-dependent
- **Trigger to Exit/Flip:** AI adoption stalls 12+ months; labor market tightens forcing re-hiring
- **Confidence:** High

### Insight 2: Advanced Packaging as AI Bottleneck
- **Claim:** CoWoS/advanced packaging capacity constrains AI compute more than fab capacity; benefits all chip designers
- **Asset Class:** Tech Equities (Semis Supply Chain)
- **Mechanism:** All AI chips (Nvidia, Google TPU, AMD, Chinese ASICs) require advanced packaging; capacity additions take 2-3 years; oligopoly pricing power
- **Expression:** Long AMKR, BESI, SNPS, Intel IFS exposure; long KLIC for chiplet assembly
- **Trigger to Exit/Flip:** Major capacity announcements; packaging yield improvements; demand destruction
- **Confidence:** High

### Insight 3: Post-Traumatic Supply Disorder (PTSD)
- **Claim:** Companies traumatized by prior overcapacity cycles refuse to add supply despite demand surge—margins expand without volume risk
- **Asset Class:** Industrials, Materials, Semis
- **Mechanism:** Screen requires: oligopoly + demand surge + capital discipline + prior trauma; GE Vernova, memory semis proved thesis in 2025
- **Expression:** Long GEV (gas turbines), FSLR (solar oligopoly if China excluded), SQM/ALB (lithium concentrated), analog semis
- **Trigger to Exit/Flip:** Capacity announcements; management guidance shifts to volume growth; new entrants
- **Confidence:** Medium-High

### Insight 4: Inference-on-Device + MediaTek
- **Claim:** Inference will migrate from cloud to device for latency (200ms vs 800ms); MediaTek positioned as TPU designer at 20x P/E
- **Asset Class:** Tech Equities (Mobile/Semis)
- **Mechanism:** Agentic AI requires real-time response; cloud round-trip too slow; memory efficiency breakthroughs enable on-device; RAM cost squeeze hurts Lenovo/Dell, helps MediaTek/Qualcomm
- **Expression:** Long MediaTek, Qualcomm; pair trade short Lenovo, Dell, Nintendo (41% BOM is RAM)
- **Trigger to Exit/Flip:** Memory efficiency breakthroughs (5 identified); RAM prices collapse
- **Confidence:** Medium
