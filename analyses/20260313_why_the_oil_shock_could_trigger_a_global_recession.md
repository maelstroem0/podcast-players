## Executive Summary

The Strait of Hormuz closure is not a standard geopolitical risk event—it is a **supply shock with no unilateral unwind**. Unlike tariffs, which Trump can reverse by tweet, a physical commodity disruption involving 90 million Iranians with asymmetric will-to-fight cannot be tacos'd away. Brent crude closed above $100 on the day of recording. Every tool in the administration's toolkit has been deployed: SPR drawdown (-172M barrels, near floor), front-month futures suppression, Jones Act exemptions—and oil is still above $100. The window before demand destruction becomes structural is closing fast; another 10 days of the straight closed "puts us in a really really bad spot."

The cross-asset map is asymmetric. **Europe and Asia are acutely exposed**; the US is relatively insulated as the world's largest oil and gas producer with export ban optionality. Japan, South Korea, and Europe entered this crisis with natural gas inventories at their lowest since Russia invaded Ukraine in early 2022. The euro is currently pricing in a rate hike—hosts call this "the most absurd thing ever"—and the re-pricing of EUR to cuts will be violent. **Dollar long is the cleanest tactical trade**: crowded short positioning + flight-to-safety demand for USD + energy independence hedge all converge. Gold faces a near-term headwind from a violent dollar rally despite being the medium-term winner (Fed QE at $500B/year of reserve management purchases, no one wants duration in a crisis).

Critically, **2026 is structurally inverted from 2022**. In 2022 you had: JOLTS at all-time highs, $2T in RRP liquidity buffer, COVID-era savings, mass immigration. Today: Goldman's underlying job growth trend is rolling over, savings depleted, zero liquidity facilities, farm bankruptcies +40% YoY, and sovereign wealth fund capex (Hormuz is the world's largest helium distributor—now offline) drying up. Every major oil supply shock in 40 years except 2022 produced a recession, and 2022 only survived because of buffers that no longer exist.

The **sleeper trade** is agricultural commodities (wheat, corn, soybeans, sugar). Farm bankruptcies at record highs have compressed supply; a spring planting season disruption due to fertilizer shortages (shipped via Hormuz) cannot be remedied mid-year—if unresolved by July/August, crop shortfalls extend 12 months. Futures positioning is still net short these assets. Historical analogues (Russia-Ukraine) show 50-300% price increases in months. No one is talking about it; everyone is chasing oil vol.

**PM implication:** Long USD via DXY or EUR/USD short (crowded positioning + asymmetric energy exposure). Long agri commodities (wheat, corn, soybeans) spot or front-month futures—spring planting risk is the key catalyst. Fade gold near-term on violent dollar rally; reload medium-term as QE and no-duration-buyer thesis plays out. Curve steepeners (long front-end, short long-end) preferred over outright bond longs—fiscal/monetary gridlock makes long bonds "dead money." Avoid equities broadly; stairstep walkdown continues as put hedges decay without monetizing and leveraged longs are slow to degross.

## 4) Regime & Scenario Analysis

- **Current Regime Fit:** Stagflation transitioning to Demand Destruction. Supply shock inflationary initially (hawkish central bank paralysis), but >$100 oil historically tips to recession; 2026 consumer/labor buffers far weaker than 2022.

- **Base Case (55%):** Hormuz partially disrupted for 2-6 more weeks → Brent $100-130 → EU/Asia slip into recession Q2-Q3 2026 → EUR reprices 3-5 cuts → dollar rallies 5-8% → Fed holds then cuts H2 2026 on global demand destruction → US recession probability rises to 50%+. Ag commodities +30-80% by summer.

- **Alt Case — Resolution (30%):** Back-channel talks succeed; Hormuz reopens within 2 weeks → oil drops to $75-80 → dollar rally fades → relief rally in equities (but structural damage to labor/consumer already done). Ag commodities rally moderates.

- **Tail Risk — Escalation (15%):** Oil hits $150-200 (Iran's stated "stop loss" level for Trump) → emergency Fed cut → dollar paradox (cut vs. flight to safety) → global recession confirmed → equities -30-40% from peak. Private credit cascade; Treasury basis trade unwind redux.

- **Anti-Thesis Trigger:** Brent sustainably back below $85 within 2 weeks; ECB delivers emergency cut before data; US fiscal stimulus (energy relief checks) announced → re-inflation without demand destruction.

- **Key timing check:** Spring planting season (April-May) is the irreversible agricultural trigger. If Hormuz not resolved by then, crop shortfall is 12-month structural, not tactical.
